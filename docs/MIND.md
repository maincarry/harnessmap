## 2. Essence

Harnessmap is a memory-and-context layer beside an AI coding or chat harness (Claude Code today, Codex since 2026-09-10). A live map of the work — topics, decisions, questions, constraints, evidence — files itself from every exchange and is read back by the agent every turn. The person governs what the agent knows with three moves: **the map** (a tree that files itself), **focus** (talk about this), **light and dim** (keep this in the background, let the agent forget that; the person still sees everything). Everything else is upkeep of those three, a plain-words way to operate them (talk to map), or protection (undo, approval). Jacob's founding sentence stands unchanged since 2026-07-17: *the user controls the flow tangibly, by understanding and prioritizing the goals of the conversation in the way they want.* Mark's framing of the value: context management by topic, through a plugin on the harness people already use, local-only, on their own subscription.

## 4. What stands, by area

### 4.1 The map and the three moves
- Everything is a node; nodes nest arbitrarily; a node carries title, statement, fit, chat memory — **ruled** M29, M36, M41.
- Three things are the product; a feature earns default visibility only if it is one of them or serves them — **ruled** M152 (Jacob, 2026-08-25 5:06 am).
- Focus = talk about this; lights the ancestor chain, which is protected from dimming — **ruled** M5, M111.
- Light = in the agent's background; dim = withheld, name only, the person still sees it — **ruled** M46, M195; the M214 minimal-line fill was withdrawn the same hour (dim means withheld; names on an offer only).
- Zoom is view-only; "dim all outside" is its own action — **ruled** M105 (supersedes Z2).
- New nodes are born lit; imports land dim — **ruled** M66, M191e.
- To-sort is a permanent system tray at the bottom; arrivals carry provenance; every item has an exit — **ruled** M51–M56, M109, M123.
- Favorites and to-tidy are pinned folders, always visible, closed by default — **ruled** M164b, M165, M167b.
- Titles ≤ 6 words and self-healing; user-facing copy says "map", never "project" — **ruled** M68, M83b, M96.
- Undo applies inverses through the guarded pipeline — **ruled** M136.
- What the agent sees, the user can see — **ruled** M20; the exact injected text stays visible until maps are shared or hosted (M163e, standing caveat).

### 4.2 Filing
- Capture everything; no topic dignity threshold; only dialogue mechanics produce nothing; a new topic always leaves at least one node — **ruled** M2, M50, M61, M73.
- Integrate, don't append; update-don't-duplicate mechanically: the round is handed the existing nodes on its subjects and rewrites them, versions kept — **ruled** M3, M204; **measured** update-suite 20/20 updates, 13/15 rewritten as current.
- Nodes state facts, never narrate the dialogue; "tell me more about X" files facts as children under X — **ruled** M67.
- The filer labels from seven types; users retype freely — **ruled** M64. Store-level enforcement — **open** (six off-list types leak; M190 rule candidate, never ruled).
- The light bounds the filer's writes; out-of-light material lands in to-sort; prompts guide, guards enforce — **ruled** M47.
- The user's words ride the system card; a glossary learned from renames is applied by a guard — **ruled** M224; **measured**: the word list changes nothing (invented title words 12%→13%); the correction loop is what works. Glossary sweep-rename — **open** (proposed 2026-09-08).
- Test 1: rulings kept 15/15 and 16/16 in every run; currency at the floor (4/5, 5/6 medians); the failure mode is bystander nodes the round did not name — **measured** M208, M210.

### 4.3 The block: what the agent receives each turn
- One block ≤ 5% of the harness window (40k chars at 200k tokens), settable; the other 95% is the host's — **ruled** M162, M212.
- Full block once per session, then deltas; re-anchored by a re-aim, 15 map events, compaction, or ⟲ refresh — **ruled** M59, M60, M195c, M195d (the pane joined the same economy).
- Tiered attention: focus at long with priority → every lit node at minimal → medium → long by warmth → the focus's full under a 40% ceiling → full for the warmest lit; no fill of dim names — **ruled** M191b, M214 addenda (2026-09-08 02:42–02:45 ET).
- The question is the consent: nodes the message names are served in full at the top, lit or set aside; kin one-liners ride along; the coverage check unfolds folded nodes and warns when rare words are absent — **ruled** M230–M232; **measured**: +0.06 over baseline, noise (M230–M232 result).
- The block is the agent's picture of what connects to what, not a lookup; the sharp block is ruled out as a product change — **ruled** M233 (Jacob, 2026-09-08 4:33 pm).
- The lighter knows the budget; an over-budget lit set is applied and fits by resolution, never trimmed by dimming — **ruled** M199 as corrected 2026-09-08 03:35 ET.
- A dim topic the question names is offered by name with a single-use token; the user's yes serves it once; light is standing curation, fetch is momentary retrieval — **ruled** M194 (3), M195k, M204.
- Agents do not pull on their own (0 recalls in 18 offers); coverage is detected mechanically — **measured** M191, **ruled** M231.
- Every host block opens with the declaration that it grants nothing and forbids nothing; the block informs a host agent and never restricts one — **ruled** M237, M238 (smoke fails on a restricting sentence).
- The map is off by default; "open map" attaches it to one session; "close map" detaches; an empty `~/.harnessmap/OFF` silences every hook without a server — **ruled** M239, M238.
- Recall test, 40k protocol, builder-written questions: map 1.65 vs transcript 0.89 on the improved v6 (kappa .77 on 414), +0.6 across five runs; the harness run at 200k with the raw transcript in every arm: map5+raw 1.22 vs raw 0.82, quarantined (kappa .67) — **measured** M208 addendum, M212/M218 addendum, docs/SCORES.md.

## 5. Tensions

1. **The rulings are argued, not isolated.** Tiered attention, the five organs, the brain, the governors, kin, coverage: each was built because it followed from the thesis, and the recall test measures only their sum. Five changes in a row sat at the noise floor, and the log has not concluded in one place that the instrument is now the limit. This document does: the next money goes to the test.
2. **The block is a picture, and pictures interfere.** Jacob's ruling that the block is the agent's view of what connects to what (M233) is right for work; the GPT incident of 2026-09-11 (a globally salient antecedent displacing the local one) is the failure a standing block manufactures. The referent test would be the first where the map can lose. Unruled.
3. **The type guard leaks** (six off-list types in live data; 17 'idea' types reached the DB in M190). Store-level enforcement was raised in M190, explained in M191e, never ruled.
4. **The map's own mind lags its design.** The playground v6 has no governors and a report three days old; the brain cycle runs on its rhythm, and nobody has fed the log's last three days into the map. The product is being run on its own record less than the record deserves.
5. **Codex is verified on the CLI and unverified on the app**, and the app is where non-coders are (Jacob, 2026-08-26: "do scholars/scientists use plugins? Are we essentially identifying coders?"). Untested since M239 on the Mac app too.

## 10. Standing guidance for the builder

From the founders' corrections, in their words where possible.

- **The founders' words are the vocabulary** — in code, schema, prompts, docs and messages; "stop inventing confusing jargons, and this is an important thing for the entire session" (Jacob, 2026-09-01). A concept they have not named gets a one-line ask or the plainest existing word.
- **Discuss before building when asked to discuss**; "Don't build" means don't; a deferral to the other founder stands until that founder rules or the first explicitly lifts it (M132 REVERSED).
- **Never present a quarantined number as a win**; write predictions before runs; say "supported / not supported" against the registered claim; separate direction from noise.
- **Explain like a scientist, in plain causal sentences** — "read NHB/JCR… learn what it is to design and explain a test" (Jacob, 2026-09-02); no fractions without meaning ("what on earth is 1.5/2").
- **Times in US Eastern and Pacific, never UTC** (Jacob, 2026-09-08). **Progress every 30 minutes while a run is live**, changed or not. **Do not report refusal counts** ("dude don't update me refusal").
- **When a founder reports the map interfering, name the cancel first, then the fix** (2026-09-09).
- **One-decision-at-a-time for visual changes** (M104 rewind); big verified batches are fine for mechanics.
- **"Ok" responses that did nothing are the most dangerous class** — the suite checks what happened, never what was claimed (M195e).
- **Restart procedure**: stop by unit and by pattern until none remains; verify the port and the new pid and build; a 200 is not proof (M232 addendum).
- **Nothing conversational goes to GitHub** (M222).
- **Test on the founders' machines is the verification**; what this box cannot run (PowerShell, the Codex app) is written from the documented format and said so.
