**0:00** Every coding session forgets.

**0:06** You spend an afternoon deciding things with your agent. Then the session compacts, or ends. Tomorrow you start blank, and so does it.

**0:20** HarnessMap keeps what was discussed. *(pause)*

**0:26** She talks to Claude Code exactly as before. Beside her, the map files the turn on its own. Nobody types into the map.

**0:40** Each node is in her own words, with her name on it. The warm-up rule is the kind of detail that gets lost by Friday.

**0:52** When she corrects the agent, the map records the correction. It does not quietly overwrite.

**1:04** One question left open, one next step named. That is the afternoon, kept. *(pause)*

**1:10** Three days later.

**1:34** Priya answered. The open question becomes a rule, and the map shows what changed.

**1:44** On the desktop, a small companion: a light that says the map is up, the current focus, and one click back to the session you were in.

**2:00** You can ask the map. It answers from its own record and tells you which node it came from.

**2:12** The same map, as a sky. *(pause)*

**2:20** Why now.

**2:26** Every engineer now works through an agent. The harness is where the thinking happens, and nobody keeps it. We do. Two lines to install, nothing changes in how you work, and it stays on your machine.

**2:44** HarnessMap. The memory layer for agent work.
